Botox

Botox is the treatment most people ask about first, and for good reason. It softens the lines that come from smiling, frowning, and raising your brows. A few small injections relax the muscles that crease the skin, so forehead lines, frown lines between the brows, and crow's feet look softer. Most people are in and out in about fifteen to twenty minutes, and there is little to no downtime. You can go back to work, pick up the kids, or head to class the same day. Results show up over a few days and settle in over about two weeks. The goal at K-Glow MD is never a stiff face. It is a rested face, one that still moves and still looks like you.

Dermal Fillers

Dermal fillers add soft volume where age, weight loss, or plain genetics have taken it away. Think cheeks that have gone a little flat, lips that could use gentle shape, or lines around the mouth that makeup keeps settling into. Fillers are gels, most often made with hyaluronic acid, a substance your skin already knows. A careful injector places small amounts where they will lift and support, not puff. You see the change right away, and it settles over the next week or two. Good filler work is quiet work. Your friends may say you look well rested, and they will not know why.

XERF

XERF is a newer kind of skin tightening that uses radiofrequency energy in a gentle, even way. There are no needles and no cutting, and most people say it feels warm and relaxing, like a hot stone treatment for your face. The heat wakes up collagen, the protein that keeps skin firm, so over the next few weeks skin can look tighter and smoother. It is a nice choice if you want firming without downtime, or if you are not ready for a more intense treatment. Many people pair it with other services for a fuller result.

LaseMD Ultra

LaseMD Ultra is a gentle laser that works on tone and texture. It can help with sun spots, uneven color, fine lines, and rough patches, and it does so with very little downtime for most people. The treatment creates tiny channels in the skin that help your own repair process kick in, and it can also help skin care products sink in better. If you have spent years in the West Texas sun, driving Loop 289 or watching games outdoors, this is often the treatment that helps your skin look clearer and brighter again. A series of sessions usually gives the best result, spaced a few weeks apart.

Potenza RF Microneedling

Potenza RF Microneedling pairs tiny needles with radiofrequency heat to rebuild collagen deep in the skin. It sounds intense, but numbing cream keeps most people comfortable, and the treatment is quick. It helps with acne scars, large pores, fine lines, and skin that has lost its spring. Afterward your skin may look pink for a day or two, like a mild sunburn, and then it starts to look smoother week by week. People who want real change in texture, not just a surface glow, often pick this one. It works on the face, neck, and other areas too.

PicoSure Pro

PicoSure Pro uses very short bursts of laser energy to break up dark spots, sun damage, and some tattoos, and to wake up collagen at the same time. The pulses are so fast that the heat stays low, which means less harm to the skin around the spot. Many people see spots fade over a few sessions, and skin looks fresher and more even. If you have tried creams for months with little change, this can be the step that finally moves the needle. Your provider will talk with you about how many visits you may need and what to expect between them.

Keralase

Keralase is for hair. It uses a gentle laser to help the scalp take in a special serum that supports thicker, fuller-looking hair. There are no shots in the scalp and no long recovery. Sessions are short, and most people go right back to their day. It is a good option for men and women who have noticed thinning at the part, the crown, or the hairline and want to act early. Hair grows slowly, so patience matters, but many people start to see less shedding and better fullness over a few months.

Titanium Laser Hair Removal

Titanium Laser Hair Removal takes the chore out of shaving and waxing. The device treats larger areas quickly and has cooling built in, so it feels far more comfortable than older lasers. Legs, underarms, bikini line, back, and face are all common areas. Hair grows in cycles, so you will need a series of visits to catch each round of growth. After that, most people enjoy smooth skin with far less upkeep. Think of all the mornings you will get back, especially in a Texas summer when shorts and sleeveless tops are everyday wear.

Skin Boosters

Skin Boosters are small injections of hydrating skin care, placed just under the surface, that leave skin looking dewy and plump from the inside out. They are not filler, and they do not change your shape. They simply feed the skin moisture and help it hold onto it. If your skin looks dull, tired, or dry no matter how much cream you use, this is often the missing step. The glow builds over a few weeks and looks very natural, like you just got back from a good night's sleep and a long drink of water.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I expect at my first med spa visit in Lubbock?

Expect a friendly chat, a close look at your skin, and a plain-English plan. You will talk about your goals, your health, and your budget, and you will leave knowing what each step does and what it costs before you agree to anything.

How long does Botox last?

Most people enjoy Botox for about three to four months. How long it lasts can shift with your dose, your muscle strength, and how active you are. A quick touch-up visit keeps the soft look going.

Do fillers hurt?

Most fillers have numbing medicine mixed in, and the team can add numbing cream too. You may feel a small pinch or pressure, but most people say it is far easier than they feared, and it is over quickly.

Can I get laser treatments if I spend a lot of time in the sun?

Yes, but timing matters. Your provider will ask about recent sun and may plan your sessions around it. Good sunscreen and a hat after treatment help protect your results in the West Texas sun.

How many Potenza sessions will I need?

Many people start with three sessions spaced a few weeks apart, then keep up with one visit now and then. Your provider will shape the plan around your scars, pores, or lines.

Is Keralase safe for all hair types?

Keralase is made to work for many hair types and scalp tones. Your provider will check your scalp, talk about your hair history, and tell you honestly if it is a good fit for you.
